# Vision

A Deadlock player gets a highly optimized build that is simple to follow.
The player does not need to understand the full item system to use it.

## Context

Deadlock has a very large number of items. The correct purchase depends on many factors:

1. Item investment. Souls spent in one category give bonus stats at set thresholds.
2. Situational items. Some items are correct only against specific heroes or conditions.
3. Net worth timing. Some items give the most value when you buy them at a specific net worth.
4. Ability leveling. Some items match the points where the hero levels a key ability.
5. Item power spikes. Some items or item combinations make the hero much stronger.
6. Hero power spikes. Each hero has times when it is most powerful and times when it falls off.

These factors interact. A player cannot calculate all of them during a match.

**The problem:** A Deadlock player does not know which items to buy, or in which order.

## Goal

Use modern algorithms to make a highly optimized build for each hero.
The build is simple and short. The player buys the CORE items from left to right.
The TIER 1, TIER 2, TIER 3, and TIER 4 items are situational options.
The player can buy one of them instead of a core item when the match makes it a better choice.
The builds go into the player's Deadlock builds in Steam.

The build gives a short idea of what to play around. It is not a full guide.
It names the key ability, the key item, and the power spike that the build depends on.
Example: Max Arctic Beam early. Run at enemies and beam them down. Escalating Exposure increases the damage.
