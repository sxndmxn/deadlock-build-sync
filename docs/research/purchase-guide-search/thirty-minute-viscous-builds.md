# Viscous: pure beam checkpoint examples

These are experimental guides. The experiment did not install them in Steam.
Buy the main sequence in order. Tier options are alternatives or later purchases; do not buy every option.
Win rates describe joint core ownership at the checkpoint, within the stated wealth state.
They do not describe the combined optional pool or prove that the build causes wins.

## Before 20 minutes: Even

**Main purchases:** Sprint Boots → Trophy Collector → Sprint Boots → Veil Walker → Extra Spirit → Improved Spirit → Mystic Burst → Tankbuster

**Final core:** Improved Spirit · Veil Walker · Tankbuster · Trophy Collector
